# Methodology

## Project Workflow

This project follows a structured end-to-end data analytics workflow to transform raw transactional data into business insights and interactive dashboards.

```
Raw Dataset
      │
      ▼
Data Understanding
      │
      ▼
Data Cleaning
      │
      ▼
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
      │
      ▼
Business Data Mart
      │
      ▼
Interactive Dashboard
      │
      ▼
Business Insights & Recommendations
```

---

# 1. Business Understanding

The project aims to evaluate the overall performance of Maven Fuzzy Factory, an e-commerce company selling stuffed animal products.

The primary business objectives are:

- Evaluate overall business performance.
- Identify the most valuable customer acquisition channels.
- Analyze customer conversion behavior throughout the purchase funnel.
- Measure product profitability.
- Analyze product refund performance.
- Generate actionable business recommendations based on data.

---

# 2. Data Understanding

The analysis begins by exploring the raw dataset to understand its structure and quality.

Activities performed include:

- Reviewing dataset size
- Checking date coverage
- Validating primary keys
- Validating foreign key relationships
- Inspecting categorical variables
- Assessing missing values
- Understanding website traffic attribution fields

---

# 3. Data Cleaning

Before analysis, several data quality issues were addressed.

Cleaning activities include:

- Standardizing string `'NULL'` values.
- Creating consistent acquisition source categories.
- Handling missing refund values.
- Standardizing date fields.
- Preparing analytical dimensions.
- Creating reusable Common Table Expressions (CTEs).

---

# 4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory analysis was performed to understand customer behavior and business performance.

The analysis covered:

- Website traffic
- Customer acquisition
- Product performance
- Customer purchase funnel
- Revenue analysis
- Profitability analysis
- Refund behavior
- Device performance
- Marketing channel performance

---

# 5. Business Data Mart

Three analytical data marts were developed to simplify reporting and dashboard development.

## mart_business_performance

Provides order-level financial metrics including:

- Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Profit
- Net Revenue
- Gross Profit After Refund
- Acquisition Source
- Device Type

---

## mart_customer_funnel

Tracks customer movement through the purchasing journey.

Includes:

- Product viewed
- Cart reached
- Shipping reached
- Billing reached
- Purchase completed

The mart also records:

- Acquisition source
- Device type
- Purchased product
- Session date

---

## mart_refund_analysis

Focuses on product refund performance.

Metrics include:

- Refund amount
- Refund rate
- Gross profit
- Net revenue
- Product refund benchmark
- Store refund benchmark
- Potential recoverable revenue

---

# 6. Dashboard Development

Interactive dashboards were built using Google Looker Studio.

The reporting layer consists of three pages:

## Page 1 — Business Performance

Provides executive-level KPIs including:

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Revenue Trend
- Product Performance
- Acquisition Performance

---

## Page 2 — Customer Funnel

Analyzes customer conversion behavior by:

- Acquisition source
- Device type
- Product viewed
- Funnel progression
- Product purchase conversion

---

## Page 3 — Refund Analysis

Evaluates refund performance across products.

Includes:

- Refund amount
- Refund rate
- Net revenue
- Gross profit after refund
- Recoverable revenue opportunity

---

# 7. Business Insights

Insights were generated by combining SQL analysis and dashboard visualization.

Key focus areas include:

- Marketing channel effectiveness
- Product profitability
- Customer conversion efficiency
- Mobile versus desktop performance
- Refund impact on profitability

---

# 8. Business Recommendations

Recommendations were developed directly from analytical findings.

Examples include:

- Improve product detail pages to increase Add-to-Cart conversion.
- Optimize the mobile purchasing experience.
- Increase investment in high-performing acquisition channels.
- Investigate products with above-average refund rates.
- Prioritize products with stronger profitability and lower refund risk.
